# Product: Newsletter Studio

## Platform

web

## Users

Strat1 consultants assembling Microsoft Partner Skilling Outlook templates for Skilling Leads, without a Microsoft 365 tenant.

## Product Purpose

Turn CMS campaign fields into a branded Outlook-ready email. Success is picking a master, editing slots, previewing the live mail, and exporting an Outlook template (.oft), an .eml draft, or a copy for Outlook that matches the Microsoft Partner Skilling look.

## Positioning

A template studio, not a general CMS and not a Copilot chat. Left side is the Power App analog. Tables are the Dataverse analog (Supabase, or local tables until a project is connected).

## Operating Context

Used on a laptop during daylight production work. Templates: Partner Template, with four campaigns (Regional Template event invite, Partner Skilling Update Template, Weekly Project Snapshot, and Custom, a free-form newsletter in the ME7 + Copilot learning paths format); Custom 2 (block by block); and Hackathons, with its campaigns.

## Brand Commitments

Microsoft Partner Skilling chrome in the email canvas (forest green, Segoe, four-square mark, Redmond footer). Studio chrome is a restrained operate surface: paper, forest green actions, Segoe UI.

## Evidence on Hand

Strat1 task note (OFT Template Maker, C-Panel/CMS input, Banner / Body / Section-1 / Footer). Screenshots of ASIA Build What’s Next, Partner Skilling Update, and Weekly project snapshot.
